HOW TO PLAY

Crossword Rules

• Fill in the white squares with letters to form words corresponding to the Across and Down clues.

• Black squares separate distinct words.

• Every letter where an Across word intersects a Down word must be valid for both clues.

Navigation & Controls

• Select a Square: Tap any white square to select it.

• Toggle Direction: Tap the selected square again, tap the DIR key, or tap the Clue Bar to switch between Across and Down.

• Typing: Use the on-screen keyboard or physical keyboard keys to enter letters. Typing advances automatically to the next square in the active word.

• Deleting: Press ⌫ (Backspace) to clear the active letter or step backward.

• Clue Navigation: Tap ◀ or ▶ on the clue bar, or use Tab / Shift+Tab on physical keyboards to cycle through clues.

Assistance Tools

• CHECK: Highlights any incorrect letters in the active word with a brief red shake.

• REVEAL: Solves and fills the correct letters for the active word if you are stuck.
