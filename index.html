      How to Play

      Objective

      Fill every white square with valid letters according to the Across and Down clues. Dark rink blocks segment the words.

      Tap any cell to set focus. Tap the active cell again or press DIR on the keyboard to alternate between Across and Down.

      Input & Correction

      Enter characters using your hardware keyboard or on-screen tactile keys. Solved cells advance the line automatically. Use Backspace to erase.
